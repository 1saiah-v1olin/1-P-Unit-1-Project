My favorite genre of music is 1000% classical music. I listen to other kinds of music, but my playlists are mostly classical.
My favorite composer is definitely Shostakovich.

  • Shostakovich was born exactly 120 years ago on September 25, 1906
  • His music primarily is for orchestra
  • He lived through the Stalinist regime, leading to many of his works to be blacklisted and not to be performed. Stalin walked out on Shostakovich's opera "Lady Macbeth of Mtsensk", then the state newspaper called the piece "Muddle instead of Music", further condemning his work

Shostakovich has some great pieces that I recommend people to listen to all the time. He has really dark pieces, and some are heavily sarcastic and playful.

  1. His 8th String Quartet is probably his most famous piece, but it is still great to listen to. This was considered Shostakovich's suicide note at the time, as he was dealing with a lot of political backlash and other setbacks in life. The whole piece is a true testiment to Shostakovich's proficiency in using limited techniques and instruments to make masterpieces
  2. His 11th Symphony truly shows his mastery of the art of painting scenes using melodies and harmonies. The 1st movement depicts the quiet morning of January 5th, 1905, painting a picture of the protesters standing outside the Tsar's palace. The 2nd movement portrays the increasing agitation of not only the crowd, but the Tsar and the crowd's insistence, leading to a massacre, known as Bloody Sunday.
